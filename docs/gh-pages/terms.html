Reporting abuse

We review every report and, where these terms have been violated, we will remove the offending content and suspend or permanently ban the account responsible — including blocking them from creating a new account to evade a ban, where we're able to detect it.

In the meantime, you can always end a connection or leave a room to stop seeing someone's content, from within the app.
